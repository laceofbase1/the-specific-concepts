02 / The Method

Most adjustments are a guess with a good outcome. This one is a measurement.

The Art of the Specific, founded by Dr. Shawn Dill and the late Dr. Andy Roberts, treats the upper cervical spine as an engineering problem: locate the deviation, correct it with the minimum specific force, prove it changed.

SPEC_01

Pattern analysis

Thermography and leg-check patterns reveal whether the atlas is holding its correction, before and after every visit.

SPEC_02

Biomechanical x-ray

Films are read to the degree. The correction vector is calculated for your spine, not a textbook average.

SPEC_03

Knee chest delivery

A solid headpiece and knee-chest position let the doctor deliver a precise, low-force correction. No twisting, no cracking.
